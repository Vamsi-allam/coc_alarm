ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

Remove account

This permanently deletes all this account's Home and Night Village timers, Lab Assistant, Builder's Apprentice, Clock Tower boosts and cooldowns, Discord destination, alert history, and saved ordering. Other accounts are not affected.

This cannot be undone. It only removes data from Builder Desk, not your Clash of Clans account.

The 22-hour cooldown starts when the boost ends. No automatic repeat.
